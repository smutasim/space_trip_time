Deep Space Trip Time Estimator

Quick, order-of-magnitude travel times across the Solar System and to nearby stars. This tool treats space like a straight road between two points and ignores gravity and orbital mechanics.

  • Geometry: straight line between objects. No gravity assists, no curved paths.
  • Acceleration mode: start at 0 km/s, accelerate (constant proper acceleration) for half the distance, then decelerate symmetrically to arrive at 0 km/s.
  • Impulse mode: two instantaneous burns (start/stop) that sum to total Δv. Coasts at cruise speed (Δv/2) between.
  • Conjunction/opposition: If both start and end are within 10 AU of each other, the result shows a range using minimum distance (|r1 − r2|) and maximum distance (r1 + r2).
  • Relativity: acceleration trips use the exact constant-proper-acceleration solution. If peak speed ≥ 1% of c, the traveler’s experienced time is also reported.

This is a simplified educational model. Real mission design must use n-body dynamics and precise navigation.

Speed must be < c. Units convert automatically.
